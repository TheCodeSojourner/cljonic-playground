Producer parameter equality (REQ-FN-014B): compares only the stored value, count, and finite-form parameters, never the produced sequence.

It is the only producer parameter-equality surface; the equal free function instead compares bounded logical observations of the produced sequences. The unbounded repeat(value) form is distinct from the finite repeat(count, value) forms even when their produced prefixes coincide, and a counted form with count zero is distinct from the unbounded form. O(1), no traversal, no allocation.
